Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Information Overview

ClusterIQ processes publicly submitted guest reviews and operational metadata strictly for competitive benchmarking, sentiment analysis, and municipal infrastructure analytics. We do not harvest, store, or sell personally identifiable private credentials.

2. Data Collection and Processing

Our data intake pipeline aggregates publicly available text records from online travel agencies and public review directories. The ingestion engine processes:

  • Public review text snippets and published star ratings.
  • Timestamp markers corresponding to published reviews.
  • Property operational category and corridor coordinates.

Any personally identifiable mentions within review bodies (such as employee names or contact details) are processed strictly for sentiment categorization and sub-issue indexing.

3. Natural Language Processing and Telemetry

Reviews submitted through the Live Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis (ABSA) interface are sanitized in-flight and evaluated using localized multilingual lexicons. Text inputs are not persisted to persistent third-party training repositories.

4. Data Security and Access Controls

All analytical payloads, cluster benchmarks, and generated municipal briefs are served over encrypted transport protocols (HTTPS/TLS). Access to raw cluster archives is restricted to authorized corridor research administrators.

5. Contact

For privacy inquiries or data governance requests regarding the Mussoorie and Dehradun Corridor review intelligence database, contact the research governance desk at info@clusteriq.internal.
